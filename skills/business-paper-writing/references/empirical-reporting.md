# Identification and economic magnitude in prose

Read this when drafting research-design or empirical-results text. Preserve the paper's organization and voice; add only the details needed to interpret its claim.

| Design | Explain to the reader |
|---|---|
| DiD | Who is treated, when exposure starts, which units supply the comparison, the parallel-trends/anticipation argument, and the estimand/aggregation the reported effect represents |
| IV | Instrument-induced variation, relevance and exclusion, the supported local/population interpretation, and inference consistent with instrument strength |
| RD | Running variable and cutoff, assignment rule, continuity argument, local population, effective bandwidth/sample, and inference convention |
| Abnormal-return event study | Event timing, return benchmark, estimation and event windows, aggregation, and event dependence |
| Text measure | Economic construct, document/section/language, scoring and aggregation rule, and validation evidence relevant to the use of the measure |

Use [design-specific diagnostics](../../shared-references/business-empirical-diagnostics.md) when determining what these outputs support. Write the identification argument from the actual source of variation. State the assumptions that define the conclusion beside that conclusion or in the design section.

For results, connect the estimate to the research question and its economic scale. Use a supported baseline, SD, IQR, or policy dose; preserve the formula and input sources in notes. Distinguish percentage points, percentage changes, basis points, and log points. A nonlinear model's marginal effect or contrast may be the quantity the reader needs.

Interpret null estimates through their interval and economically meaningful range. Preserve mixed findings and consequential sample changes across models. Compare different methods on aligned outcomes, units, horizons, and target populations; agreement or divergence requires an actual comparable contrast.

Contribution prose names the economic question answered and the verified increment relative to close studies. For style cleanup, remove generic self-evaluation and procedural filler only when the remaining sentence preserves attribution, modality, negation, conditions, and evidence strength. Author examples and the existing confident-prose contract govern rhythm and voice.
