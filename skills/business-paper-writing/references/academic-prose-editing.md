# Academic prose editing: lessons from econ-humanizer and plus

Read this for a requested academic prose cleanup, removal of generic AI-like phrasing, or a concrete style problem found while writing. Preserve the original argument, voice, and organization. Editorial patterns are prompts to inspect meaning; a word or punctuation mark does not establish AI authorship.

## Preserve the claim before editing

For each changed sentence, retain facts, attribution, negation, modality, scope, assumptions, comparison direction, units, aggregation, time window, numbers, formulas, citations, and null or mixed findings. Shorten only when the revised sentence still means the same thing.

Do not add a named policy, institutional detail, number, identification strategy, or robustness result to make vague prose concrete. Use verified source material when available; otherwise preserve the uncertainty or flag the actual gap. If correcting a substantive error is authorized, distinguish that correction from tone editing.

## Patterns and useful revisions

| Pattern | Inspect and revise |
|---|---|
| Promotional self-description | Replace unsupported praise of the contribution with the actual finding and increment. A documented substantive claim stays. |
| Meta-prose and throat-clearing | Delete openings such as `It should be noted that` when the remaining statement is complete. Keep an explanation that changes how the evidence should be read. |
| Repeated contribution formulas | State the contribution once where it is needed; use later paragraphs to develop the distinct result or reasoning. |
| Methods padding | Replace a sentence that only announces a regression or table with the result, specification change, or comparison the reader needs. Retain the exhibit reference. |
| Synonym cycling | Use one stable term for the same construct, policy, sample, or estimand. Keep different labels when they denote different objects. |
| Vague attribution | Name the verified source and its supported claim. Do not invent a reference or present a disputed claim as established. |
| Empty transitions | Connect paragraphs through the specific shared question or evidence. A necessary contrast or causal connection remains. |
| Repeated rhetorical scaffolds | Reorganize only where paragraphs repeat a template without distinct supporting content. Lists remain when they carry actual parallel information. |
| Ambiguous `this` or noun stacks | Name the referent when unclear and unpack nominal chains into readable clauses. Preserve technical construct definitions. |
| Uniform or overpacked sentences | Split or combine where it helps comprehension, preserving qualifications and logical connections. Follow the author's natural rhythm. |
| Inconsistent coauthor voice | Keep person, tense, terminology, and interpretation consistent across the affected sections. A local edit does not authorize rewriting the whole paper. |

## Words and phrases to inspect

Inspect `groundbreaking`, `pivotal`, `profound implications`, `novel and robust`, `shed light on`, `pave the way`, `multifaceted`, `delve`, `utilize`, `it is worth noting`, `we conduct a regression`, and generic `the literature suggests` phrasing. Replace or remove them only when they add no substantiated meaning.

Respect technical usage: financial leverage, robust standard errors, statistical significance, extensive margins, pass-through, accruals, and conservatism are substantive terms when used correctly. A meaningful technical term should not be replaced merely because it appears on an upstream word list.

## Sentence-level craft

Shortening `the results show that` or a similar reporting clause must preserve attribution, the exhibit reference, and the interpretation's scope. A statement about what a source reports must remain attributed to that source after editing.

Name an ambiguous `this`; clarify a model or condition with `in which` when `where` obscures its referent. Use conventional compound-modifier hyphenation where needed for comprehension and the journal's style. Give Greek symbols meaningful names and preserve their definitions. Apply these small edits to actual ambiguity or inconsistency, without changing a construct or estimand to obtain a more fashionable field term.

## Qualification and punctuation

Remove redundant stacked hedges while retaining the claim's actual uncertainty. In particular, removing `suggests`, `is consistent with`, or `may` can strengthen a mechanism or causal claim. A hedge is not filler simply because the abstract contains it.

The two upstream humanizer modules disagree on em dashes. Follow the user's punctuation choices and the manuscript's voice; do not ban or insert punctuation to imitate or conceal authorship. Parentheses and semicolons are useful when they express the intended relationship. Sentence lengths have no required quota.

## Section-specific judgment

- **Introduction:** make the question, finding, and verified contribution clear; preserve the author's motivation and order unless restructuring was requested.
- **Literature and hypotheses:** synthesize relevant findings and mechanisms, preserving disagreements and the exact scope of cited evidence.
- **Empirical results:** prioritize sample, units, coefficients, intervals, specification changes, and claim level over stylistic variety.
- **Conclusion:** state the economic answer and its supported implications. Avoid promotional summaries and stock future-research filler.

## Example with unchanged evidence

Before:

> It is worth noting that disclosure quality is positively associated with investment efficiency (beta = 0.12, 95% CI [0.04, 0.20]).

After:

> Disclosure quality is positively associated with investment efficiency (beta = 0.12, 95% CI [0.04, 0.20]).

The association, estimate, interval, and variables remain the same. If the original says the evidence is consistent with a monitoring mechanism, the edit must retain that interpretation's modality rather than state that monitoring caused the result.

Completion means the requested text is revised and its factual and logical meaning is preserved. Explain material editorial choices when requested; do not add a separate audit artifact or a claim that the text will evade AI detection.
