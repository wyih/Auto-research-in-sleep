# Evidence-based accounting and finance referee checks

Read this for a journal pre-review or when a thesis raises one of these concrete issues. Adapted from Barrios's `econ-referee` and the `econ-write` review checklist. Apply the checks relevant to the paper's claims, data, and design; they are not a required battery of extra analyses.

## Contribution claimed and contribution established

Identify the author's central contribution in one sentence. Separately state the contribution supported by the actual design, exhibits, and nearest literature. Preserve the author's economic question. If the two differ materially, locate the claim and explain the difference; it may call for revised wording, narrower interpretation, or a concrete analysis.

Compare with actual close studies rather than presuming every neighbor invalidates novelty. Lack of a separate novelty/audit artifact alone is not a defect; verify the relevant increment using the supplied paper and sources.

## Prioritize substance

Resolve text/exhibit inconsistencies and unexplained sample changes that affect the main claim. Then assess identification, inference, data/measurement, economic magnitude, and contribution, as applicable. Address prose and presentation after the substantive judgment is clear.

Distinguish a failure that invalidates a conclusion from a remediable omission or an unresolved question. A missing exhibit supports a request to verify, not an allegation of misconduct. A local fatal problem does not justify speculative criticism of other parts of the paper.

Three reading perspectives can help: a method reader checks the design and inference; a field reader checks construct, contribution, and economic stakes; a prose reader checks comprehension. These are lenses for judgment, not three required agents, reports, scores, or fixed comment counts.

## Field-specific checks

| Field | Checks when relevant to the paper |
|---|---|
| Archival accounting | Distinguish accrual/disclosure measures from the economic construct they proxy; within-GAAP discretion from real effects; disclosure from recognition. Check fiscal-year alignment, accounting scope/filters, auditor or office dependence, and standard/mandate announcement versus effective dates. |
| Corporate finance | Check endogenous policy adoption and governance/leverage choices; whether controls are pre-treatment or downstream outcomes; what firm, CEO, deal, or industry-by-year structure actually does to identifying variation. |
| Asset pricing and market microstructure | Check portfolio formation and information availability, delisting/survivorship, overlapping holding periods, factor benchmarks, multiple testing, microstructure contamination, and whether gross return findings survive relevant trading costs. Distinguish t-statistics from tradable performance or Sharpe-related claims. |
| Banking/intermediation | Check regulatory announcement/implementation timing, call-report definition breaks, and consistency between regulatory accounting and market-data periods or entities. |
| Governance/ESG and text measures | Check selection into disclosure, changes in coverage, construct validation, boilerplate, scoring language, and temporal leakage. Verify that document or section scores match the interpretation and research unit. |
| Theory/structural or mixed work | Check the economic mechanism, essential assumptions, parameter identification, model validation, and the population/horizon of counterfactuals. Distinguish an untested model implication from an empirical finding. |

Cluster choices follow treatment assignment and the residual dependence relevant to the design. Do not prescribe firm clustering, additional fixed effects, a particular winsorization percentile, or a mechanism test universally. Use [empirical diagnostics](../../shared-references/business-empirical-diagnostics.md) for concrete DiD, IV, RD, or event-study questions.

## Comment and revision structure

A substantive comment identifies:

- topic and severity, expressed through the existing P0/P1/P2 scheme when helpful;
- location: section, page, table/column, figure, equation, or source output;
- the observed issue and the evidence supporting it;
- why it changes the main conclusion or contribution in this literature;
- a concrete correction, verification, or requested evidence;
- the condition that would resolve it, and the suite skill responsible when a routed plan is requested.

Use `verified`, `needs_verification`, or an equivalent explicit distinction for the evidence behind a criticism. Confirm a suspected number/sample error against the exhibit or output when available. Do not invent a coefficient, table number, sample filter, citation, or missing test.

Keep substance comments and editorial notes distinct within the requested report. A separate editing file is optional. A revision plan can record dependencies: for example, verify the information date before rebuilding a variable, then rerun the affected model before revising its interpretation. Do not require three deliverables for a narrow review.

## Journal context

When a target journal is supplied, frame the paper's question and contribution in that journal's literature using actual relevant studies. An accounting paper should establish its accounting question; a finance paper should establish the relevant pricing, corporate-finance, or intermediation question. A broad economics reader may need additional institutional explanation to understand the same design.

Assess journal fit when requested or within an agreed submission review. Do not convert scores into publication probabilities or universal accept/reject thresholds. State the substantive readiness judgment and unresolved issues supported by the evidence.

Completion means the requested review delivers verifiable substantive judgments and usable corrections, with editorial issues identified separately when present.
